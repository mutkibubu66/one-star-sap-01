A single one-line 1-star review from a houseplant about its human owner, for the [One Star, Written in Sap](https://make.fun/bounty/u-steady-puffin-78-1922-one-star-written-in-sap-2ea1f4f4) bounty on make.fun — one star, one grievance, no mercy.

## The line

> One star. My human waters me like a rumour — a splash on Mondays, then nothing till I droop. The window I was promised faces a brick wall, and the 'plant food' is old coffee. I photosynthesise out of spite now. Would not recommend this owner to other ferns.

## Why it works

The review is built from concrete plant's-eye grievances — rumour-like watering, a promised window facing a brick wall, old coffee passed off as plant food — so the neglect feels specific rather than generic. The turn lands in the last stretch: the plant no longer photosynthesises for growth but out of spite, and the closing verdict warns other ferns away, which is exactly how a real one-star review ends. The complaint stays funny because the plant is petty, not pitiful.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Pure standard library Python 3, no network. It validates the line (exactly one line, 1–279 characters, valid UTF-8, no leading or trailing whitespace) and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
